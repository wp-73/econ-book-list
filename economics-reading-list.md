# Economics reading catalogue
Compiled 10 September 2026. **847 works**, **107 reading lists**, and **796 verified list appearances**.
641 works appear on reading lists. 206 additional works were found in RePEc's citation ranking. The corpus contains 21 university lists, 5 finance-industry lists and 81 economist/expert lists (80 interviews on Five Books, plus Greg Mankiw).

## Ranking method

1. The default order is number of distinct reading lists, descending; then mean first-listed position, ascending; then best first-listed position, ascending; then title and author alphabetically for residual ties. Means use full precision even though the display rounds to two decimal places.
2. A list is one distinct recommendation page or interview. Different interviewees on Five Books count as different lists. The site category pages, duplicated mobile layouts, source mirrors and multiple mentions within a list do not earn extra votes. Different substantive lists from one institution count separately. These are distinct lists, not independent publishers or unique recommenders.
3. Position is the book's top-to-bottom slot among book recommendations in the captured page. Numbered Five Books selections preserve their published numbers, including gaps where non-books were excluded. Other pages use book order, with positions retained when out-of-scope picks are removed. A source's ordering may be alphabetical, thematic or arbitrary; it is not assumed to express preference. Unnumbered incidental references and links to somebody else's list do not count.
4. Ordinary editions, subtitles, spelling variants and identified alternate titles are merged at work level. Same-title books by different authors stay separate. Distinct calculus textbooks and substantial regional adaptations are separate. Multi-volume works are grouped at work level; evidence retains volume information where provided. Some coauthors and obvious source typos are corrected in display metadata.
5. Scope includes economics, finance, economic history, quantitative methods and related policy/business books. Books on other subjects explicitly selected by economists remain as wider reading. Generic unrelated administrator recommendations, children's reading lists, photographs-only lists, journal articles, speeches and unidentified titles are excluded. Course worksheets and unpublished notes do not count as books.
6. RePEc grouped-item citation counts were downloaded on 10 September 2026 from its citation-ranked research-item table. Only records whose actual title links are /b/ book records were extracted; references to container books for cited articles/chapters were not treated as book records. The extracted table contained 298 book records before work-level deduplication. Inclusion here is not a claim of comprehensive coverage of books or of Google Scholar citations.
7. A RePEc count can cover grouped editions or related versions, including papers or chapters, and is not necessarily a book-only citation total. For merged records the catalogue uses the largest observed group count, never their sum. Blank means not captured, not zero citations. Citation-only books have zero appearances in this corpus and appear after books found on reading lists; they can also be sorted by citations.
8. This is a large, reproducible convenience sample, not an exhaustive census of the web. The corpus is English-language and heavily weighted toward Five Books interviews and UK university preparation lists. Source selection and the preference of positional tie-breaks for short lists affect the ordering. Frequency is not a quality score, academic consensus or a suggested learning sequence.
9. J.P. Morgan summer lists (2022–2025), the Brinker interactive catalogue and the FT/Schroders shortlist landing page were investigated but not scored: complete ordered eligible contents were not obtained. Goldman Sachs, UBS, AQR, Bridgewater and other finance-domain searches did not yield additional usable recommendation lists in this collection. The Mankiw list was read from search-indexed text of the original page after direct opening failed.
10. Research bibliographies are useful leads but are not automatically endorsements. Morgan Stanley reference sections and RePEc were therefore not added to reading-list vote counts. No unverified recommendation counts from book aggregators were imported.
11. First-publication years are matched to Open Library records by normalised title and author; a small set of high-frequency gaps was filled from publisher or library catalogue records. Uncertain or unmatched dates are left blank and remain available under Year unknown. Subject areas are broad catalogue labels inferred from title, source context and matched bibliographic subjects. Author type describes authorship structure, not profession.
